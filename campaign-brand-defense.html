Brand defense

A Search build for tightly defined branded demand, using controlled query coverage, a CPC ceiling, trust-led messaging, and a documented QA path.

CampaignGoogle Ads SearchMarketUnited StatesDepth5-page document
Portfolio exercise, not client work. The campaign and platform screens are staged examples and do not represent verified Apple campaign performance.

Case at a glance

  • QuestionHow should branded purchase intent be protected without mixing it with broader discovery traffic?
  • DecisionUse exact and phrase match, campaign-level exclusions, a €1.50 CPC ceiling, and official-store messaging.
  • ArtifactA paused campaign build with keyword groups, campaign controls, sitelinks, and launch checks.
  • Evidence limitThe build and indicators are demonstrations; they are not verified Apple campaign results.

Technical depth

  1. Query controlReview search terms for reseller, support, discount, and informational drift before expanding coverage.
  2. MeasurementRequire a validated purchase event and compare branded impression-share loss by rank and budget.
  3. Next stepLaunch with approved brand rules, then evaluate query quality, CPC, conversion rate, and incremental value.
