Negative keyword library

A three-level method for blocking irrelevant searches, protecting exact-match traffic, and keeping queries in the intended campaign or ad group.

Scope: E-commerce, DTC & high-ticket retailPlatforms: Google Ads & Microsoft AdvertisingLevel: Account protection

Three levels of exclusions

  1. Shared account exclusion listsApplied across campaigns for zero-intent and informational noise, employment and HR traffic, support, servicing, repairs, and DIY searches.
  2. Campaign-level sculpting listsApplied to generic and shopping fleets for cross-campaign routing, exact-match protection, and Performance Max brand-exclusion control.
  3. Ad-group match-type isolationExact-match negatives are applied to phrase and broad ad groups so the intended ad group receives the query.

Match-type execution SOP

Applying the wrong negative match type can block valuable traffic. The syntax and protection profile must match the intended exclusion.

Negative match typeSyntax exampleBehaviorIdeal application
Negative exact[free macbook pro]Blocks only the exact query sequence without additional words.Fine-grained query sculpting while protecting high-intent variations.
