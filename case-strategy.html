This portfolio exercise splits search demand into five campaign layers so brand, product, competitor, professional, and automated traffic can be reviewed separately.

Why the structure works

  • Intent separation: brand, flagship, competitor, professional, and automated growth demand are not treated as one undifferentiated auction.
  • Budget accountability: each layer can be reviewed against its own role rather than against an account-wide average.
  • Query routing: the architecture creates a foundation for campaign-level and account-level exclusions.
  • Channel clarity: Performance Max supplements the portfolio instead of obscuring the logic of search acquisition.
