Performance Max portfolio growth

A controlled cross-channel extension to the Search portfolio, covering feed setup, asset-group logic, creative inputs, and audience signals.

Case at a glance

  • QuestionHow should Performance Max add reach without obscuring the demand already owned by Search?
  • DecisionUse a governed feed, one coherent asset group, complete creative coverage, and audience signals as inputs.
  • ArtifactMerchant setup, asset-group configuration, creative inventory, sitelinks, and audience signals.
  • Evidence limitThe case shows controllable inputs, not incremental lift or verified production performance.

Technical depth

  1. Portfolio controlProtect the Search architecture with brand rules, exclusions where available, clean conversion goals, and feed governance.
  2. MeasurementCompare conversion value, new-customer quality, channel overlap, and incrementality rather than Ad Strength alone.
  3. Next stepValidate the feed and tracking, establish a baseline, then test the campaign with an approved budget and holdout logic.
