Case at a glance

  • QuestionHow should professional and business intent be separated from general consumer hardware traffic?
  • DecisionKeep URL control, combine exact and phrase intent, and use business-specific assets and exclusions.
  • ArtifactAutomation controls, keyword architecture, technical RSA copy, negative taxonomy, and a paused build.
  • Evidence limitThe case demonstrates campaign construction and does not claim qualified leads or sales.

Technical depth

  1. Intent modelGroup enterprise, developer, creator, fleet, and procurement themes without sending retail traffic into the route.
  2. MeasurementDefine qualified-lead or purchase value before comparing this higher-value segment with consumer Search.
  3. Next stepConfirm landing-page capability, sales follow-up, audience policy, and lead-quality feedback before launch.
