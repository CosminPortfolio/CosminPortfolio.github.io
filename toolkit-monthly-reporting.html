Pre-flight data QA checklist

  • Billing reconciliation: Google Ads UI spend matches dashboard and invoice totals within 0.5%.
  • GA4 transaction match: e-commerce purchase counts reconcile with backend or CRM sales without double counting.
  • UTM check: no missing campaign tags or unexplained Unassigned traffic spikes.
  • Conversion-tag verification: core Google Ads conversion actions show Active status and recent event hits.
  • Dashboard sync: Looker Studio sources have refreshed without broken fields or join errors.

A focused 30-minute review

  1. 00:00–00:05 · Top-line health checkReview spend, revenue, blended ROAS, and goal attainment.
  2. 00:05–00:15 · Campaign breakdown & optimizationsCover wins and losses across brand defense, flagship, and switcher campaigns.
  3. 00:15–00:22 · Experimentation & copy testingReview completed A/B tests and agree on the next hypotheses.
  4. 00:22–00:30 · Next-month action planApprove bid adjustments, budget reallocations, and priorities.
