Case at a glance

  • Export modesDaily partitions support scheduled analysis; intraday tables support lower-latency monitoring.
  • Residency decisionThe Google Cloud dataset region is treated as a technical and data-policy choice.
  • Warehouse useThe case identifies attribution, LTV, CRM joins, BI, and ML preparation as downstream use cases.
  • Honest statusThe document shows the setup surface; it does not claim a completed production BigQuery link.
