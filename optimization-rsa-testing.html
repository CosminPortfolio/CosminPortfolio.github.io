RSA messaging experiment

A two-page experiment plan comparing a price-led message with a switching-friction message, including asset inventory, test thresholds, illustrative results, and rollout rules.

DocumentOptimization deliverable 02StatusSandbox simulationDepth2-page document
Portfolio exercise, not client work. Identifiers are removed and demonstration figures remain clearly labeled.

Case at a glance

  • Single questionDoes friction-reduction messaging improve conversion performance relative to a price anchor?
  • Creative inventoryThe matrix maps five headlines and two descriptions to control or challenger concepts.
  • Decision guardrailsCTR and CVR require a meaningful lift while CPA and ROAS protect the commercial outcome.
  • Demonstration resultThe 30-day figures are illustrative and do not represent a live employer test.

Technical depth

  1. ArtifactA test brief with one hypothesis, controlled message matrix, primary metric, guardrails, and rollout rule.
  2. Failure checksUneven allocation, overlapping changes, insufficient power, asset-delivery bias, and a CPA trade-off hidden by CTR.
  3. Next stepUse a campaign experiment where clean allocation is required and pre-register the stopping rule.
