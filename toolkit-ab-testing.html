A/B testing playbook

A working process for writing a hypothesis, planning the sample, checking the result, and recording what happens next.

Platforms: Google Ads Experiments, GA4, Optimizely / VWOTarget: 95% confidenceScope: RSA, extensions & CRO
Portfolio examples, not employer outcomes. The experiment IDs and results are demonstration material from the supplied playbook.

Four-stage experimentation lifecycle

  1. Hypothesis & variant isolationDefine one variable, such as price anchor versus friction-free migration, and keep control and challenger bid caps equal.
  2. Traffic & sample-size validationTarget at least 2,000 impressions and 100 conversions per variant, with a minimum 14-day run covering two weekly cycles.
  3. Statistical evaluation & runtimeMonitor CTR, CVR, CPA, and Ad Strength daily; require the selected confidence threshold before action.
  4. Decision & rolloutScale a meaningful winner, close an inconclusive test, or pause a challenger with materially worse CPA or CVR.

Winner

p < 0.05 and a meaningful CVR improvement: promote and document the winning concept.

Inconclusive

Delta below 3%: end the test and pivot toward a genuinely different message.

Loser

Material CVR decline or higher CPA: pause the challenger and log the learning.

What happens after the test

If the challenger wins

  1. Update the master copy bank with the winning hook.
  2. Promote the winning variant to the approved traffic allocation.
  3. Verify that any headline pinning retains Good or Excellent Ad Strength.
  4. Align the landing-page H1 with the winning message.

If the challenger loses or remains inconclusive

  1. Return traffic to the control.
  2. Record the rejected hypothesis so subtle variants are not repeated.
  3. When p > 0.10, test a genuinely different proposition rather than minor copy edits.
