Case at a glance

  • Funnel modelThe dashboard places top-, mid-, and bottom-funnel counts in one review surface.
  • Scenario signals66.6M impressions, 727.8K clicks, and 830.3K conversions are demonstration values.
  • InterpretationConversion totals above click totals are treated as an event-stacking question, not hidden.
  • Decision outputThe case proposes budget, mobile-experience, and event-governance actions.

Technical depth

  1. ArtifactA journey dashboard joining traffic, device behavior, macro conversions, and stacked micro-conversion events.
  2. Failure checksDuplicate purchase IDs, event inflation, cross-device breaks, mobile friction, and rates distorted by event counting.
  3. Next stepAudit event definitions, reconcile purchases, and separate diagnostic micro-events from the commercial outcome.
