# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The portfolio serves hiring reviewers who need to establish credibility quickly and technical reviewers who want to inspect the decisions, implementation logic, and evidence behind the work.

## Product Purpose

Present Cosmin Pintilie's hands-on performance-marketing, measurement, optimization, and reporting work in a form that is fast to scan and substantial enough to evaluate. Success means a reviewer can understand the breadth of the portfolio, open any case without leaving the homepage, and distinguish demonstrated skills from verified client results.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects campaign architecture, measurement planning, ongoing optimization, and executive reporting as one operating system rather than presenting isolated marketing artifacts.

## Operating Context

The work uses Google Ads, GA4, GTM, BigQuery, and Looker Studio. Case studies use staged platform screens and demonstration data to show account structure, tracking governance, analysis, and decision-making.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages.
- Case studies are browsed through one homepage gallery with one full case open at a time.
- URL hashes provide direct links to disciplines and individual cases.
- The résumé remains downloadable as a PDF; case-study PDFs are not part of the visitor experience.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Cosmin Pintilie name, supplied portrait and résumé, direct first-person voice, and the incumbent dark visual system.

## Evidence on Hand

Sixteen hands-on case studies cover strategy, measurement, optimization, and reporting. They include staged Google product screens, illustrative datasets, technical specifications, decision frameworks, and explicit evidence limitations. No Apple account access, live employer performance, or independently verified commercial outcomes may be implied.

## Product Principles

- Make the work itself the primary experience.
- Let a reviewer scan first and continue into technical depth without changing pages.
- State evidence limits beside the claims they qualify.
- Prefer native, accessible web content over document viewers and duplicate routes.
- Remove navigational choices that do not add new information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, semantic headings, responsive reading layouts, and meaningful image alternatives are required.
