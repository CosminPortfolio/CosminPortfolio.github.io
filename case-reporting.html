SEM Fleet Executive Center

The command surface consolidates multi-year Search and Performance Max data from 2018–2024 into one governed view.

  • Four executive KPIs for rapid fleet-health assessment.
  • A 30-day revenue-versus-traffic scaling trajectory.
  • A device-level revenue breakdown showing 64% mobile dominance in the demonstration dataset.
  • A color-density campaign-accountability matrix for quickly locating outliers.

ROAS & budget pacing monitor

The financial-intelligence surface translates $613.52K in scenario spend and $7.6M in scenario revenue into a governed calculated field:

SUM(Conversion Value) / SUM(Cost)

A dual-axis monthly trajectory and pacing heatmap support decisions to freeze sub-1.0× ROAS spend and redirect budget toward the scenario’s strongest Performance Max drivers, including a cited 54.62× segment.

Funnel attribution & micro-conversion pathway

The full-funnel surface traces 66.6M top-of-funnel impressions to 727.8K mid-funnel clicks and 830.3K bottom-of-funnel conversions in the supplied demonstration dataset.

Top of funnel

66.6M impressions establish reach and auction exposure.

Mid funnel

727.8K clicks show progression into active consideration.

Bottom of funnel

830.3K conversions reflect event stacking in the scenario, including campaigns with conversion rates above 100%.

The source analysis also identifies 70.6% mobile conversion-completion dominance and explicitly treats multiple conversion events per user journey as a reporting interpretation issue.
