Case at a glance

  • Event scopeProduct Category registers item_category for event-level product analysis.
  • User scopeCustomer Status registers customer_type for persistent cohort and audience use.
  • Reporting useBoth dimensions become available in GA4 Explorations after registration.
  • Activation useThe user-scoped property can support audience building when consent and data policy allow it.

Technical depth

  1. ArtifactA registration map connecting raw parameters to event-scoped and user-scoped GA4 definitions.
  2. Failure checksScope mismatch, naming drift, high-cardinality dimensions, and properties collected without valid consent.
  3. Next stepConfirm values in DebugView, wait for processing, and test the dimensions in Explorations and audiences.
