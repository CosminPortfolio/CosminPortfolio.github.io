Workstream: search-term audit

The scenario reviews 1,200 raw queries and identifies high-spend, zero-conversion intent. Examples include free, macbook repair, and galaxy s23 case, which are routed into governed account-level exclusion lists.

Workstream: responsive-search-ad testing

The test compares two genuinely different reasons to buy instead of changing a few words.

Concept A: price anchor

Trade-in value and financial incentive messaging intended to reduce perceived purchase cost.

Concept B: frictionless migration

Data-transfer and switching-ease messaging intended to address buyer anxiety.

The decision uses CTR and conversion rate together. CPA remains the commercial guardrail; Ad Strength is treated as a setup check, not proof of performance.

Workstream: auction-response memo

The executive memo responds to a 15% Lost Impression Share (Rank) drop attributed in the scenario to aggressive Samsung promotional activity in the flagship-search auction.

  • Identify whether the movement reflects rank, budget, or a broader demand shift.
  • Review quality scores, ad relevance, landing-page alignment, and bid strategy before increasing spend.
  • Translate the findings into focused actions and approval requirements for leadership.
